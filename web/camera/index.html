Face check

    How it works

    Each heartbeat changes skin colour very slightly. The camera watches your forehead and cheeks and reads your pulse from that change. This is a risk indicator, not a diagnosis.

    • Hold still for 30 seconds and face a steady light.
    • If signal quality is low, you will be asked to retry.
    • Less reliable with movement, dim or flickering light, and on darker skin in poor light. Not validated in microgravity.

    Face check optional

    A little extra insight, only if you want it.

    Off

    While the camera reads your pulse, it can also notice how your face is moving today compared with your own usual. You stay in charge.

    • What it looks atBlinks, how open your eyes are, your brows, and how much you move.
    • It never guesses feelingsNo labels like happy or sad. Only "like your usual" or "different".
    • It stays with youWorked out on this device. No photo or video is kept, only a few numbers.
    More about how it is used

    It is one small extra input for your stress indicator, for medical and safety use only. It is not shared unless you choose to share your daily summary. You can turn it off or delete the numbers at any time, here or in Settings.
