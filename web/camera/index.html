    How it works

    Each heartbeat changes skin colour very slightly. The camera watches your forehead and cheeks and reads your pulse from that change. This is a risk indicator, not a diagnosis.

    • Hold still for 30 seconds and face a steady light.
    • If signal quality is low, you will be asked to retry.
    • Less reliable with movement, dim or flickering light, and on darker skin in poor light. Not validated in microgravity.

    Facial-behaviour check optional

    Off

    During the same 30 seconds, the app can also measure how your face behaves and compare it with your own earlier checks. It is one small extra input for the stress indicator.

    • What is measured: blink rate, eye openness, brow lowering, how much your face and head move.
    • What is not: emotions. The app never labels how you feel.
    • Processed on this device. No video or image is stored, only the numbers.
    • For medical and safety use only. Not shared unless you choose to share your daily summary.
    • You can turn it off here or in Settings at any time, and delete the stored numbers.
