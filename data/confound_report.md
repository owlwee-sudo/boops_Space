- **R² from mission day alone**: share of the score a straight line over mission day explains. 0.5 or more would mean the calendar explains most of it.
- **Follows the day**: after shuffling the records across days, how well the new score on day d matches the real score on day d. 0.8 or more would mean the shuffle reproduced the score.
- **Follows the data**: how well a record keeps its score when it moves to another day. Multi-day rules (streaks, 3-day and 7-day averages, the baseline window) are broken by the shuffle, so this cannot reach 1.0.
