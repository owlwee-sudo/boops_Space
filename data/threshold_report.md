# Harm-weighted thresholds

A miss costs 5, a false alarm costs 1 (ratio 5). Starting values, not clinically validated. Generated by `python engine/thresholds.py`.

## WESAD stress model (real labelled lab data)

| Model | Threshold | Catches stress | False alarm rate | Cost | At 0.5: catches / false alarm rate / cost |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| wrist_all | 0.32 | 93% | 10% | 182 | 87% / 7% / 251 |
| daily | 0.13 | 98% | 12% | 108 | 90% / 4% / 176 |

Thresholds come from leave-one-person-out predictions on WESAD (15 people, lab stress on Earth). Not validated for spaceflight. `STRESS_MODEL` stays `"rules"` by default.
