1. Medical & Flight-Safety Disclaimer

Prototype · Not a medical device. BOOPS was created as a demonstration project for the NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026. Nothing in this website, the downloadable Android APK, the desktop/local workstation, the camera check utility, or the underlying repository constitutes medical advice, clinical diagnosis, treatment, prophylaxis, or aerospace flight-medicine certification.

  • Risk indicators only: BOOPS presents statistical risk indicators and early warnings derived from baseline z-scores and literature-inspired relationship weights. It does not diagnose infections, cardiovascular disease, musculoskeletal pathology, or radiation sickness.
  • Emergency checklists: The 6 emergency protocols bundled in content/emergency_protocols.json (and displayed in the high-contrast red/white emergency views) and 7 instrument guides in content/tool_guides.json are uncertified educational prototypes. Always follow official mission flight rules, flight-surgeon guidance, and local emergency medical services in real life.
  • Camera pulse & HRV caveats: Browser rPPG pulse estimation is sensitive to lighting and motion, suppresses estimates when quality falls below 0.50, and records any camera HRV with hrv_confidence: "low" without feeding clinical risk baselines.

2. Simulated & Reference Data Disclosure

Because public day-by-day astronaut longitudinal clinical telemetry is not available, the offline BOOPS app and downloadable CSV/PDF exports are preloaded with 540 synthetic crew-day records across three fictional crew members (A1, A2, A3) over 180 simulated mission days. All generated telemetry rows carry an explicit Simulated badge in both the database export and UI.

  • Radiation event modeling: Simulated solar particle event timing and profile shapes are informed by public space-radiation patterns from NASA OSDR RadLab and NOAA SWPC GOES satellite datasets, rather than live mission telemetry.
  • Simulated camera signal: When running the repository locally (python engine/server.py), opening /camera/?sim=steady feeds a deterministic synthetic RGB waveform so reviewers can verify the rPPG pipeline without a webcam. Simulated camera logs are tagged and kept separate from real camera sessions.

3. Privacy, Offline Storage & On-Device Camera Policy

BOOPS is engineered around crew-first privacy and zero runtime cloud dependencies. In the downloadable Android APK, local workstation, and this static site:

  • No server-side collection of user logs: Check-ins, camera measurements, emergency logs, and sharing toggles are stored strictly on your device (local SQLite data/mission.db or browser local storage ahm.log_entries). No health logs are uploaded to any cloud server.
  • Zero third-party cookies or analytics trackers: This website, Legal page, and the offline BOOPS application include zero advertising scripts, analytics beacons, or third-party tracking cookies.
  • In-memory camera processing: When you grant webcam/camera access in the offline app's /camera/ utility, MediaPipe Face Landmarker and POS rPPG algorithms run locally on-device using vendored WASM and model files. Raw video frames are never recorded, saved to disk, or transmitted over the network.
  • Opt-in facial behaviour & zero emotion labels: Optional facial-behaviour change tracking is off by default (FACE_WEIGHT = 0.0, hard-capped at 0.15), compares only within-person drift (low / medium / high) against the user's own first 14 camera sessions, and never classifies or infers emotions (consistent with Barrett et al., 2019 and EU AI Act safeguards against workplace emotion recognition).
  • Crew-first sharing defaults: In the app's privacy model, crew members see alerts first; raw sensor data sharing defaults to off, daily summaries are user-toggleable, and only safety-critical emergencies default to shared.
  • No external requests: The home page, this legal page, the Evidence Map and the offline BOOPS app load no fonts, scripts or styles from other servers. The Inter font is self-hosted, and pages fall back to system fonts when it is missing.
